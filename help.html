💳 Subscriptions & Refunds

How to cancel my subscription?

All subscriptions are managed by Apple via the App Store. You can cancel at any time by following these steps:

  1. Open "Settings" on your iPhone/iPad.
  2. Tap your name (Apple ID) at the top.
  3. Tap "Subscriptions".
  4. Select AlbumAI and tap "Cancel Subscription".

Refund Policy

Since all payments are processed by Apple, developers cannot issue refunds directly. If you need to request a refund:

  • Please visit reportaproblem.apple.com.
  • Sign in with your Apple ID and select the subscription to report a problem.

❓ FAQ

Q: Does AlbumAI require an internet connection?
A: Yes, an internet connection is required for the AI to analyze photos and generate text.
Q: How do I restore my Premium purchase?
A: Go to the subscription/upgrade page in the App and tap the "Restore Purchase" button to regain access to your subscription.
Q: Is my data private?
A: We take privacy seriously. Your photos are analyzed by AI solely for generating text and are not shared publicly without your permission.
Q: What if generation fails?
A: If generation fails due to network issues, it typically won't count against your limit. If you experience persistent issues, please contact support.
